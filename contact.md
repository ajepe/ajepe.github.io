## Start with the database

If you're planning a migration — or suspect you need one — send me a few lines about what you're running. Rough is fine; I'll ask the follow-up questions.

### What to include

- **Your current version** (13, 14, 15, 16?) and, if you know, where you'd like to end up
- **Rough size** — users, database size, how many companies or websites
- **Custom modules** — how many, who built them, whether the source is available
- **Integrations** — what Odoo talks to: shop, accounting, carriers, in-house systems
- **What worries you** — downtime, data loss, a past migration that went badly

## How it usually goes

### A first call

We talk through what you're running, what's in the way and what a good outcome looks like. This is where I figure out whether I'm the right fit.

### An assessment

For migration work, this is the real starting point: I audit the custom modules, database size, integrations and risks, and you get a written plan with a fixed quote.

### Staging, then cutover

The upgrade happens on a staging copy first, with you testing as long as you need. The cutover itself is rehearsed, scheduled around your business, and reversible.


## Questions people often ask

### How soon can you start?

It depends on what I'm working on, but usually within 2 to 4 weeks.

### Do you work with clients in other time zones?

Yes. I work with clients all over the world. We find meeting times that suit both of us and handle the rest in writing.

### How do you structure a project?

For migrations: assessment, staging upgrade, verification, then a rehearsed cutover. For everything else, short iterations with a demo at the end of each one. On small jobs that can all happen in a week.

### Can I talk to past clients?

Yes. After our first conversation I can share references and examples from similar projects — including the 14 → 16 migration behind the case study.

### What happens after the migration?

I offer ongoing support for bug fixes, checks and small improvements. Migrations also tend to surface performance work, which I'm happy to quote for separately.


## Other work

Not everything is a migration. I also take on:

- **Custom development** — modules and automations the standard apps don't cover
- **Integrations** — connecting Odoo to your shop, carriers, accounting tools or in-house systems
- **Performance** — finding out why things are slow (queries, config, code, crons) and fixing it
- **Consulting and support** — code reviews, architecture advice, and ongoing help once a project is live
